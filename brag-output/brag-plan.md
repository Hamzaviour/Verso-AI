# Brag Plan: Verso

## What is this app?
Verso clusters coverage of each news event from 33 outlets, plots who covered it on a left-to-right bias spectrum, compares headlines word by word, and separates facts every side reports from claims only one side makes.

## The angle
A calm, editorial product commercial that teaches by showing. Every scene is the real product doing its job on a real story from today's wall (the DOJ / Alaska Senate lawsuit, 8 outlets). The voice walks the viewer through how to use it: pick a story, watch the spectrum, compare headlines, read agreed facts, play the headline game, check your bubble.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Three real headlines about the same event slam in one after another, each tagged with its outlet and tinted by its plate color (cyan left, ink center, magenta right). VO: "Same story. Eight newsrooms. Eight very different headlines."

## Key moments
- Two misregistered plates (cyan, magenta) slide together; the overlap goes solid ink and becomes the Verso mark.
- Outlet plates slide from the center baseline to their place on the spectrum (beat-locked at 18.56s).
- Compare toggle flips on; process-yellow highlighter wipes across the words only one side chose; a loaded term gets its plainer alternative.
- Agreed facts tick in first, then "Only on the left" claims.

## Outro / punchline
Dark ink band, the mark, "Read the other side." VO: "Verso. Read the other side."

## User flow worth showing
Today's wall, then click a story, then the spectrum fills, then compare headlines, then agreed facts, then the headline game and the bubble meter.

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with app-store feature clarity)
- Creative direction: editorial product film for a news tool; quiet confidence, print-shop details
- Interpretation: long enough holds to read real headlines, soft slides and crossfades, one strong reveal per scene, restrained SFX.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 52s
User explicitly asked for a full commercial explaining what the product is and how to use it, with voiceover. That needs about 42s of narration, so this run deliberately exceeds the usual 15-25s brag window.

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #e6e6e1 (newsprint), sheets #f3f3ef, outro #131314
- Text: #131314
- Accents: cyan #0086c3 (left), magenta #c8006a (right), process yellow #ffe14a (highlighter)
- Display font: Source Serif 4 (bold, optical sizes)
- Body/UI font: Archivo (variable width; wordmark at 112% width, weight 800)
- Strongest visual element: outlet plates on the spectrum; double-ruled small-caps mastheads on front-page slabs; the registration rule (cyan/key/magenta thirds)

## Share copy (draft)
Same story, eight newsrooms, eight headlines. Verso puts every side of the news on one page, and shows you what they all agree on.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed under narration
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady, clean, 110 BPM)
- Music treatment: fade in over 1s, sit around 0.13 under the voice, lift to ~0.32 in the final 3s after the last line, fade out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read (assets/music/cues, vol-12). Strong cues: 18.56s (spectrum plates start), 22.93s, 24.56s. Beat grid ~0.545s; sequential text reveals use every other beat or natural timing for readability.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the registration rule and the outro mark glow breathe with music RMS. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched (card-place for headline slams, soft drop for plates landing, switch for the compare toggle, mouse click for the story click and quiz reveal, one bell for the logo)
- Restraint rule: never mask the voice; SFX at 0.45-0.6.

## Voiceover script
1. Same story. Eight newsrooms. Eight very different headlines.
2. This is Verso. It reads thirty-three outlets, from left to right, and puts every side of a story on one page.
3. Start on today's wall. Every card is one event, and the dots show who's covering it.
4. Open a story, and every outlet slides onto the spectrum. By lean, and by how reliable it is.
5. Flip on compare. The words only one side chose light up, and loaded language gets a plainer alternative.
6. Then, the facts. What every side reports comes first. What only one side mentions gets labeled. Computed, not guessed.
7. Play the headline game to test your instincts, and see how balanced your own reading really is.
8. Verso. Read the other side.

## Storyboard (timed to the generated narration)

### Scene 1 — Same story — 0.0-5.0s
Three headline slabs slam in (Mother Jones, The New York Times, Breitbart) with their real headlines. Plate bar on each.
Sequential/interaction: yes, 3 slabs one by one (~0.55s apart), all held together after.
Audio: card-place per slab. Transition: soft slide → 2.

### Scene 2 — Reveal — 5.0-12.6s
Cyan plate slides in from the left, magenta from the right, overlap goes ink: the mark. Wordmark "verso" and the line "The other side of every story." "33 outlets · left to right" appears below.
Audio: soft bell when the lens lands. Transition: crossfade → 3.

### Scene 3 — Today's wall — 12.6-18.4s
Browser frame, /today. Story grid with 4 real neutral headlines and coverage dot strips. Cursor moves to the lead card and clicks.
Sequential: cards fade up in order. Audio: mouse click. Transition: zoom-through → 4.

### Scene 4 — The spectrum — 18.4-25.6s
Lead story title. Spectrum panel: 8 outlet plates slide from the center to their place (beat-locked 18.56s). Axis labels Left / Center / Right, reliability lines.
Audio: soft drops on first and last plate. Transition: slide → 5.

### Scene 5 — Compare headlines — 25.6-33.2s
Row of 4 front-page slabs. Compare toggle flips on; yellow highlights wipe across distinctive words; "Decertify" gets a wavy underline and a tag "plainer: invalidate".
Audio: switch on toggle. Transition: slide → 6.

### Scene 6 — Agreed facts — 33.2-41.6s
"Every side reports" panel: 2 agreed claims tick in with outlet chips. Then "Only on the left" card with 1 claim.
Audio: drop per panel. Transition: crossfade → 7.

### Scene 7 — Game and bubble — 41.6-47.6s
Left: quiz card, slider puck moves right, reveal shows Breitbart, plate slides to its spot. Right: bubble meter bars grow, Balance number.
Audio: click on reveal. Transition: wipe to dark → 8.

### Scene 8 — Outro — 47.6-52.0s
Ink band. Mark, "Read the other side." in large serif, small line "verso".
Audio: music lifts, final fade.

**Music mood:** steady, warm, editorial.
**Audio summary:** a quiet bed under a friendly guide voice, small print-shop clicks and drops on real UI moments, and the music opening up for the last line.
